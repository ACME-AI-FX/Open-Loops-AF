Open Loops

1 · Let's get you connected

Open Loops reads the messages you send on Slack and email, and reminds you when someone hasn't replied. It needs to see those accounts — a one-off. The ticks below update by themselves; you don't need to press anything to refresh them.
Open Claude opens a black window. If it asks you to sign in, follow the link. For Slack or Gmail, type /mcp in that window, press Enter, pick Slack or claude.ai Gmail, choose Authenticate, and click Allow in your browser. If they're already connected there's nothing to do here — the ticks will appear within a minute.

2 · Who's who?

So nudges hit the right note, tell it who's senior to you, a peer, junior, or outside the company. It's looked at who you message most and taken a guess — correct anything that's wrong, then press Save. You can change this later in ⚙ Settings.

Your name

  
First name, as you'd sign a message.

  
 

 

  
Your AI

  
Which AI assistant reads your messages and writes the nudges. It must be installed and signed in on this computer — after switching, the Home tab re-checks the connections.

  
 

 

  
History

  
How far back the AI reads when building context — the "who's who" people scan (both people you write to and real people who write to you), and the very first loop scan. Longer = fuller picture, slower scans. To re-scan with a new window: press "Pick from who I message most" below, or Start over for a full rebuild.

  
 

 

  
Daily refresh time

  
Weekdays, only while you're logged in. Runs the refresh, then the timer (if enabled). Pick something before you'd normally open the page.

  

     
  

 

 

  
Chasing

  
  
Internal email domains (comma-separated). Anyone else with an email is external; Slack-only contacts count as internal.

  
  

   Send instead of draft
   
Unticked = chase lands as an unsent draft in the original thread and you press send. Ticked = it is sent immediately in your name, unseen.

   
   
  

  

   Timer (auto-chase)
   
Runs with the morning refresh. Any loop still waiting after N workdays (from the ask, or from the last chase) gets chased automatically — drafted or sent according to the boxes above. Each card gets an auto: on/off switch so you can exclude a particular message.

Start over

  
Wipes the list, the people picks and the learned tone, and takes you back to step 1 as a brand-new user would see it. Keeps your name, domains and tone settings. Slack/Gmail stay connected.

  

 

 

  
Your voice

  
Reads how you actually write to each person (your own Slack DMs + sent email, read-only) and stores a per-person style note with real examples, so chases sound like you. Runs automatically after Who's who; re-run whenever you change people.
